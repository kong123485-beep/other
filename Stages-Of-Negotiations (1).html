Stages of Negotiations 談判階段

After looking at the vocabulary that can be used in negotiations, we will learn some phrases that can be used in different stages of negotiations to make our position clear and help achieve the best possible outcome.睇完談判可以用嘅詞彙之後，我哋會學吓喺談判唔同階段可以用嘅句子，講清楚自己立場，爭取最好嘅結果。

✅ 已齊晒 11 個階段，中英對照版 — 英文上面，中文解釋下面，方便直接溫書。
1. Starting and Outlining Your Aims and Position 開始及表明目標和立場
  • I'd like to begin with a few words about our general expectations...
    我想先講幾句關於我哋嘅整體期望……
  • May I outline our principle aims today...
    可唔可以等我講吓我哋今日嘅主要目標……
  • There are two main areas that we'd like to concentrate on / discuss.
    有兩個主要範疇，我哋想集中討論。
  • What are we really talking about here...
    其實我哋而家傾緊嘅係咩呢……
  • We want to clarify our position...
    我哋想講清楚我哋嘅立場……
  • We have to decide...
    我哋要決定……
2. Proposing 提出建議
  • There are several options...
    有幾個選擇……
  • Would it be possible...
    可唔可以…… / 有冇可能……
  • How do you feel about...
    你點睇……？
  • Do you think you could consider...
    你會唔會考慮……？
3. Responding to Suggestions 回應建議
  • As far as your proposal is concerned, we think that...
    就你哋嘅建議嚟講，我哋認為……
  • May we offer an alternative? We propose that...
    我哋可唔可以提出另一個方案？我哋建議……
  • We'd like to make an alternative proposal. We propose that...
    我哋想提出一個替代方案，我哋建議……
  • From where we stand, a better solution might be...
    喺我哋嘅角度睇，更好嘅解決方法可能係……
  • Now that you mentioned it...
    既然你提到呢點……
4. Agreeing 同意
  • This is a fair suggestion.
    呢個係一個公平嘅建議。
  • That's probably all right.
    咁應該都 OK。
  • Provided that you... / As long as you... we will...
    只要你哋……，我哋就會……
  • You have a strong point there.
    你呢點講得好有道理。
  • I think we can both agree that...
    我諗我哋雙方都可以同意……
  • I don't see any problem with / harm in that.
    我唔覺得咁做有咩問題 / 有咩壞處。
5. Disagreeing 唔同意 / 反對
  • That would be really tough for us.
    咁對我哋嚟講真係好難。
  • Take another look at the numbers... Budgets are tight...
    再睇多次啲數字啦……預算真係好緊……
  • I am afraid that is out of the question.
    恐怕呢個係冇可能嘅 / 免談。
  • If you look at it from our point of view, ...
    如果你企喺我哋嘅角度睇，……
  • Is that your best offer?
    呢個係你哋最好嘅條件喇？
  • The main obstacle to progress at the moment seems to be...
    目前阻礙進展嘅主要問題似乎係……
6. Clarifying 澄清 / 講清楚
  • Does anything I have suggested / proposed seem unclear to you?
    我頭先建議嘅嘢，有冇邊 part 你覺得唔清楚？
  • I'd like to clarify our position.
    我想講清楚我哋嘅立場。
  • Are you suggesting that...
    你係咪即係話……？
  • Could you clarify one point for me?
    可唔可以幫我講清楚一點？
  • Could you be more specific?
    可唔可以講得具體啲？
  • Can we summarise your position up to this point?
    我哋可唔可以總結一下你到目前為止嘅立場？
7. Compromising 讓步 / 妥協
  • How flexible can you be on that?
    喺呢方面，你哋有幾大彈性？
  • May we offer an alternative?
    我哋可唔可以提出另一個方案？
  • We feel there has to be a trade-off here.
    我哋覺得呢度一定要有啲取捨 / 交換。
  • What do you think is a fair way to resolve the situation?
    你覺得點樣先係一個公平嘅解決方法？
  • If you could... we could consider...
    如果你可以……，我哋可以考慮……
  • On condition that we agree on... then we could...
    條件係如果我哋就……達成共識，咁我哋就可以……
  • Let's think about the issue of...
    等我哋諗吓……呢個問題。
  • Could we tie this agreement to...?
    我哋可唔可以將呢個協議同……掛鈎？
8. Delaying 拖延 / 爭取時間
  • We would have to study this. Can we get back to you on this later?
    我哋要研究吓呢點，遲啲再覆你得唔得？
  • We'll have to consult with our colleagues back in the office. We'd like to get back to you on it.
    我哋要返去同公司嘅同事商量吓，之後再覆你。
9. Refusing a Proposal 拒絕建議
  • I am afraid we couldn't agree to that.
    恐怕我哋唔能夠同意呢點。
  • I'm sorry, we can't accept that.
    唔好意思，我哋唔能夠接受。
  • I'm afraid your offer doesn't go far enough.
    恐怕你哋嘅條件仲未夠。
  • I'm sorry, but we must respectfully decline your offer.
    唔好意思，我哋要鄭重婉拒你哋嘅提議。
  • That would be very difficult for us because...
    咁對我哋嚟講會好困難，因為……
  • Unfortunately, we must decline your offer for the following reasons...
    好可惜，基於以下原因，我哋要拒絕你哋嘅提議……
10. Accepting a Proposal 接受建議
  • We are happy to accept this agreement.
    我哋好樂意接受呢個協議。
  • This agreement is acceptable to us.
    呢個協議我哋可以接受。
  • I think we have a deal.
    我諗我哋成交喇。
  • I am willing to work with that.
    我願意就咁樣合作。
  • It sounds like an alternative / option / possibility...
    聽落係一個替代方案 / 選擇 / 可能性……
  • That sounds reasonable.
    聽落好合理。
11. Closing / Summarising 總結 / 收尾
  • I'd like to run through the main points that we've discussed.
    我想快速重溫一次我哋討論過嘅重點。
  • Can we summarise the proposals in a few words?
    我哋可唔可以用幾句說話總結啲建議？
  • Let's look at the points we have agreed on.
    等我哋睇吓我哋已經同意咗啲咩。
  • So the next step is...
    咁下一步就係……
  • Now, let's just take a moment to review what we've discussed.
    而家等我哋花少少時間，回顧吓討論過嘅嘢。
  • So, I'll summarise the important points of our offer.
    咁等我總結一下我哋提議嘅重點。
